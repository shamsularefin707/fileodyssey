Quarterly river report

This report covers the river valley survey for the spring quarter. Levels rose in every month until May, then began to fall.

Water levels

MonthLevel (m)Note
March2.1Clear water after the frost
April2.9Rose after three days of rain
May2.4Falling slowly
June2.2Low and warm

The highest reading came from the bend below the mill, where the new sandbank has pushed the current against the eastern bank. The keeper says the change began after the winter floods.

Wildlife

Nobody expected the count of herons to be so high. Eleven birds were seen in one stretch of reeds, more than in any survey since the records were first kept.

Recommendations

The committee recommends a second survey in autumn and a new marker below the mill.
