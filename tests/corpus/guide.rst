=================
Ferry timetable
=================

The ferry crosses the river every half hour during the day. Tickets are
sold on board.

Weekday crossings
=================

+---------+----------+
| Leaves  | Arrives  |
+=========+==========+
| 07:00   | 07:20    |
+---------+----------+
| 07:30   | 07:50    |
+---------+----------+

See the `harbour office <https://example.org/harbour>`_ for holiday times.

* Bicycles travel free.
* Dogs must be on a lead.

.. note::

   The last crossing leaves at 21:30.
