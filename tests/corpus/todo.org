#+TITLE: Survey plan

* Before the survey
  Check the boats and pack the field books.
** Equipment
   - Measuring pole
   - Waterproof notebook
* During the survey
  Record the level at every marker. See [[https://example.org/markers][the marker list]].

  | Marker | Distance |
  |--------+----------|
  | A      | 0 m      |
  | B      | 250 m    |

* After the survey
  Type up the notes the same evening, while they are fresh.
